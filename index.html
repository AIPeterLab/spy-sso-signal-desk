Decision Path

1

Use the completed SPY close

Calculate SMA200 and 20-day annualized SPY volatility after the market close.

2

Set the SMA200 trend state

Above SMA200 × 1.01 turns risk-on. Below SMA200 × 0.99 turns risk-off and targets 100% Cash.

3

Inside the bands, keep the trend

Between the upper and lower bands, retain the prior risk-on or risk-off trend state.

4

Apply volatility when risk-on

At or below 15% → 100% SSO. Above 15% through 25% → 50% SSO / 50% SPY. Above 25% → 100% Cash.

5

Use the target next trading day

Any SMA200 or volatility threshold change after the close creates a rebalance signal for the next trading day.
